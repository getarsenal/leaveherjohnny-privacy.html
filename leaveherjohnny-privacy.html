Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Leave Her Johnny mobile application ("Application"), developed by Scheidel Holdings LLC ("we", "our", or "Service Provider"). The Application is provided as a free service and is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. It does not require an account, sign-up, or login of any kind, and it does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

All game data — including your progress, ship upgrades, gold and resources, statistics, achievements, and settings — is stored locally on your device.

We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on external servers.

Local Data Storage

All data created while playing the Application is stored locally on your device using your device's standard local storage. You are solely responsible for managing your saved game data.

Network Use

The Application runs entirely on your device and does not transmit your information to us or to any third party. The Application does not connect to our servers, and we have no ability to access your saved game data.

Third-Party Services

The Application does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties. Because no data is collected, there is no data to share.

Data Retention

All data remains on your device unless you choose to delete it. Clearing the Application's data or uninstalling the Application will remove locally stored game data.

Children's Privacy

The Application does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the integrity of the Application. Because data is stored locally on your device, the security of that data also depends on your device's own protections.

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
